# 🏏 Indian Cricket Legends: Career Stats Analysis (Power BI Project) 📊

Welcome to my Power BI Data Analysis project! 

As a massive cricket fan, I built this interactive dashboard to celebrate and analyze the extraordinary careers of three of India's greatest cricketers: **Rohit Sharma**, **Virat Kohli**, and **MS Dhoni**. 

This project uses Power BI to turn their raw career numbers into an interactive visual story, comparing their batting milestones, boundary-hitting abilities, and match-winning accolades.

## 📖 What is this project about?

This Power BI report is a deep-dive statistical comparison of the "Big Three" of Indian cricket. Instead of just looking at plain numbers, this dashboard allows users to interactively explore how these legends performed over their careers. 

From tracing their journey to massive centuries (**100s**) and half-centuries (**50s**), to analyzing their boundary blitzes (**4s and 6s**), and celebrating their "Man of the Match" and "Player of the Tournament" awards—this dashboard visualizes what makes them legends.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights (What you will see)

My Power BI dashboard includes several interactive pages and visuals focused entirely on these three players:

*   **🌟 Player Career Overview:** Head-to-head comparisons of total runs, matches played, and batting averages for Rohit, Virat, and Dhoni.
*   **🏏 Milestone Masters (50s & 100s):** Visual breakdowns of their consistency. See exactly how many centuries and half-centuries each player has scored across formats.
*   **💥 Boundary Blitz (4s & 6s):** Analysis of their aggressive batting. Compare who hits more fours and who clears the ropes more often (6s).
*   **🏅 Awards & Glory:** Visualizing their "Man of the Match" and "Player of the Tournament" awards, highlighting their clutch performances in crucial games.
*   **📈 Career Timeline:** Tracking their performance trends over the years to see their peaks and consistency.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

To build this interactive report, I used the following Power BI features:

*   **Power Query:** For cleaning, transforming, and shaping the raw career statistics of the three players before loading them into the model.
*   **Data Modeling:** Creating a clean data structure to ensure accurate filtering between players, match formats, and years.
*   **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Writing custom formulas to calculate dynamic measures like Total Runs, Strike Rates, Total 4s/6s, and Century Conversion Rates.
*   **Interactive Visuals:** Using slicers (to select specific players), cards, bar charts, and donut charts to make the data engaging and easy to compare.
